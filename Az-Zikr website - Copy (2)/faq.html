Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding admissions, rules, and course details.

Are classes really 100% free?

Yes, all courses provided by Az-Zikr International are completely free of charge Fi-Sabeelillah.

Who can enroll in these classes?

Our courses are exclusively for female students worldwide. Select courses in the Kids Tarbiyah department are open to young boys and girls (ages 8-16).

How do I register for a course?

Visit the Enroll Now page, click on your desired course flyer, and join the official WhatsApp registration group link provided.

What platforms are used for classes?

Classes are conducted live via Zoom or Google Meet, with study materials and announcements shared in dedicated WhatsApp groups.
