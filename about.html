About Az-Zikr International

Providing authentic Islamic education to sisters across the globe Fi-Sabeelillah.

Our Mission

Az-Zikr International is a free online Islamic institute dedicated exclusively to women. Our goal is to make authentic Quranic and Islamic knowledge accessible to female students worldwide, without financial barriers.

Academic Excellence & Standards

We maintain a rigorous academic evaluation process across all departments to foster dedicated learning and student accountability. Our grading criteria includes:

  • Grade A: 90% – 100% (Outstanding)
  • Grade B: 80% – 89% (Excellent)
  • Grade C: 70% – 79% (Very Good)
  • Grade D: 60% – 69% (Good / Pass)
  • Grade F: Below 59% (Fail)

Student Safety & Rules

We strictly enforce student privacy and security in our learning community. Unofficial WhatsApp groups, unauthorized advertising, and personal contact among students are strictly prohibited to ensure a safe learning space.
