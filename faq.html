Frequently Asked Questions

Find detailed explanations regarding admissions, study formats, and institute policies.

Are classes really 100% free of charge?

Yes, absolutely. All courses, study guides, live instruction, and digital reading materials provided by Az-Zikr International are completely free Fi-Sabeelillah (for the sake of Allah).

We do not charge any admission fees, monthly tuition, or hidden registration costs across any of our academic departments.

Who is eligible to enroll in these courses?

Our institute primary focus is providing a safe, supportive, and dedicated virtual learning environment for female students worldwide of all skill levels.

However, select foundational courses offered under our Kids Tarbiyah Department are specially structured for young children (both boys and girls ages 8–16).

How does the registration process work?

Enrolling in a batch is quick and straightforward:

  • Navigate to the Courses page to select your desired program.
  • Click on the course flyer or enrollment button to access the official WhatsApp admission group.
  • Follow the short orientation instructions shared by group administrators to confirm your seat.

Which digital platforms are used for classes and materials?

We utilize user-friendly online tools to ensure smooth learning across different time zones:

  • Live Classes: Conducted interactively through Zoom Workplace or Google Meet.
  • Announcements & Recitations: Managed through moderated, official WhatsApp study cohorts.
  • Self-Paced Resources: PDF textbooks, Tajweed workbooks, and recorded video links are accessible via our website portal and E-Library.

What are the student privacy and security rules?

We uphold strict privacy standards to safeguard our sisters:

Creation of unofficial messaging groups, unauthorized commercial advertising, or personal side-messaging between students without admin consent is strictly forbidden and results in immediate removal from the institute.
