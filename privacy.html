Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect student information securely.

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Az-Zikr International. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our students. This Privacy Policy outlines what minimal information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your information when you interact with our free online Islamic institute.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate enrollment and manage our classes, we only collect the essential information required for communication and class organization:

  • Full Name: Used for attendance and academic records.
  • Contact Number: Used via WhatsApp to coordinate class schedules and announcements.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We respect your data privacy. All student information (such as names and contact numbers) is retained only for the duration of the active course and is securely deleted once the course is completed.

4. Minors and Guardian Consent

For courses that include younger students, such as our Kids Tarbiyah Department, we strictly require that minors join and participate exclusively through their parents' or legal guardians' phones. By utilizing a guardian's device, explicit parental supervision and authorization are established.

5. Third-Party Platforms (WhatsApp & Zoom)

Our classes and study circles operate using third-party communication tools, primarily WhatsApp and Zoom. Participants should be aware that when joining class groups or live sessions, profile names, phone numbers, and profile visibility may become accessible to other members within that specific group. We advise users to adjust their personal privacy settings on those platforms accordingly.
