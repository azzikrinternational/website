Level 3 – Linguistic Depth

This level is designed for students who have completed foundational studies and wish to master the advanced structure and linguistic depth of Qur'anic Arabic. The courses focus on Tarkeeb (syntactic analysis), Tajzia (grammatical breakdown), and the application of Arabic grammar to Qur'anic text.

  • Talelaat - JUZ 01 (Live Classes at Zoom)
