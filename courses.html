Seerah Department

Ar-Raheeq al-Makhtum

This course offers an in-depth study of the life of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), based on the award-winning biography Ar-Raheeq al-Makhtum by Safiur Rahman Mubarakpuri. Students explore the noble character, mission, challenges, and triumphs of the Prophet (ﷺ) — gaining a comprehensive understanding of his Seerah as a timeless guide for personal, social, and spiritual reform[cite: 20].

Urdu Medium Online

Ummahatul-Mu'minin

This course focuses on the lives of the noble wives of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) — the Mothers of the Believers. It highlights their virtues, sacrifices, and contributions to the preservation and transmission of Islam. Through their stories, students learn about strength, wisdom, faith, and devotion, drawing inspiration for moral and spiritual growth in their own lives[cite: 20].
