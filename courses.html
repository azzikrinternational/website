Our Departments & Courses

Comprehensive, structured Islamic education across six specialized departments.

Qur'anic Arabic Language Department

Offers a complete journey from foundational Arabic grammar to advanced linguistic analysis and research — empowering students to understand the Qur'an in its original language with clarity and depth.

Level 1 – Foundation Stage

Designed for beginners to build a strong base in Qur'anic Arabic and grammar through easy and guided sessions.

Curriculum Focus:
  • Asaan Arabic Grammar (Google Form)
  • Certificate Course (Objective Type)
  • Online Lisan ul Quran
  • English Lisan-Ul-Qur'an (Based on the book and lecture series of Lisan ul Quran in Urdu)
  • Professional Diploma Program
Urdu Medium Online

Level 2 – Intermediate Stage

Aimed at students ready to deepen their understanding of Qur'anic language and structure through prompt assistance and interactive learning.

Curriculum Focus:
  • Super Diploma Program
  • Tarkeeb Kaise Karain
  • Lisan Ul Quran: Basic to Advance (on Zoom Live Classes)
Urdu Medium Online

Level 3 – Linguistic Depth

This level is designed for students who have completed foundational studies and wish to master the advanced structure and linguistic depth of Qur'anic Arabic. The courses focus on Tarkeeb (syntactic analysis), Tajzia (grammatical breakdown), and the application of Arabic grammar to Qur'anic text.

Curriculum Focus:
  • Muntakhib Nisaab: Tarkeeb & Tajzia
  • A'raab al-Qur'an (Surah Analysis)
  • Talelaat - JUZ 01 (Live Classes at Zoom)
  • Talelaat (Live Classes at Zoom)
  • Durus al-Lughah al-'Arabiyyah – Book # 03
  • Grammatical Analysis – JUZ 30 (Live Classes at Zoom)
  • Degree Program — a combination of DLA-3 and A'raab al-Qur'an
